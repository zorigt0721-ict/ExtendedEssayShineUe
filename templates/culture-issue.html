A broader cultural trend or social issue examined across multiple English-language sources.

Research Question 1

"How has the 'Body Positivity' movement in UK-based fitness advertisements influenced the evolution of marketing terminology and consumer perception of 'health' between 2010 and 2024?"

Define the "Body Positivity" movement. Explain why the UK fitness market was chosen and establish the 14-year timeframe.

Detail the "Diachronic Study" (comparing two different time periods). You will analyze 5 ads from 2010 and 5 ads from 2024 for comparison.

Compare old terms (e.g., "shred," "weight loss," "perfection") with new, inclusive terms (e.g., "movement," "wellness," "empowerment").

Discuss how the "visual language" of the ads (lighting, model diversity) has shifted to match the textual changes.

Discuss how British societal values regarding inclusivity have forced companies to change their linguistic strategies.

Final judgment on whether this is a genuine linguistic shift or just "performative marketing."
