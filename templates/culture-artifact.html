Culture (Single Socio-cultural Artifact)

Analysis of a specific item (movie, song, advertisement) and its impact on an English-speaking culture.

Research Question 1

"How does the visual and lyrical language in Childish Gambino's 'This Is America' utilize intertextuality to critique systemic racism in 21st-century American culture?"

Introduce the artifact (the music video). Define "intertextuality" and your thesis: the video uses symbols to highlight the distraction of entertainment from real violence.

Provide context on US racial tensions and the history of racial caricatures (like Jim Crow) as they relate to the video's imagery.

Analyze visual symbols (the cars, the specific poses, the background chaos) as a "visual language."

Contrast the upbeat "African-inspired" melodies with the aggressive, violent lyrical interruptions. Analyze specific metaphors like "Gucci on my indoor."

Discuss the reception of the video by the English-speaking public and its role in the modern cultural conversation.

Evaluate if the artifact successfully used language and imagery to provoke a specific cultural shift in the US.
