Language (Linguistics Focus)

How the English language evolves or is used within a specific group or platform.

Research Question 1

"To what extent has the emergence of 'Corporate Speak' and the use of linguistic euphemisms in Silicon Valley tech companies (2015−2025) altered the perception of professional transparency?"

Define "Corporate Speak" and "Linguistic Euphemisms." Establish the specific geographical focus (Silicon Valley) and the timeframe (10 years). State the thesis regarding how language is used to mask corporate accountability.

Explain the "Corpus Selection." For example, analyzing the last 5 years of "Year in Review" letters from CEOs of Google and Meta. Detail the linguistic markers being tracked (e.g., passive voice, nominalization).

Provide a history of "The Professional Register" in English and how it has shifted from formal to "pseudo-casual" in the modern tech industry.

Analyze the use of specific words like "right-sizing" or "pivoting" and how they function to obscure negative news.

Discuss how the target audience (employees and shareholders) interprets this language versus its literal meaning.

Summarize how this linguistic shift impacts trust in corporate communication and provide a final answer to the RQ.
