Literature (Literary Analysis)

A deep dive into a literary work originally written in English.

Research Question 1

"How does Tennessee Williams use the 'Southern Belle' linguistic register in 'A Streetcar Named Desire' to represent the psychological disintegration of Blanche DuBois?"

Introduce the play, the author, and the character of Blanche. Thesis: Her overly formal, archaic language is a shield against her trauma and a sign of her mental break.

Explain the decline of the "Old South" (aristocracy) versus the "New South" (working-class industrialization) in New Orleans.

Analyze specific dialogue, including her use of French phrases, poetic hyperbole, and formal "politeness" markers as social defense.

Contrast her language with Stanley's "proletarian" and aggressive English. Show how this "clash of registers" creates the central conflict.

Connect the linguistic decay (Blanche losing her ability to maintain "proper" speech) to her eventual madness.

Summarize how Williams uses language as a tool for characterization and tragedy.
