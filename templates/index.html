What is English B EE?

The English B Extended Essay (EE) is a 4,000-word independent research project that explores the English language or culture through one of four distinct categories.

Each category has specific structural requirements, research question formats, and grading criteria based on the IB rubric (5 criteria, 34 marks total).

Grading Criteria

  • Criterion A (Focus/Method): 0-6 marks
  • Criterion B (Knowledge): 0-6 marks
  • Criterion C (Critical Thinking): 0-12 marks
  • Criterion D (Presentation): 0-4 marks
  • Criterion E (Engagement): 0-6 marks

Total: 34 marks (Grade A = 30+)
